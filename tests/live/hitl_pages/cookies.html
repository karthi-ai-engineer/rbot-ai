7 tips for brewing better coffee at home

☕

Great coffee doesn't need an expensive machine. These small habits make the biggest difference.

Tip 1: Buy whole beans

Ground coffee goes stale within days. Whole beans keep their aroma for weeks if stored in an airtight jar.

Tip 2: Grind just before brewing

A simple burr grinder gives an even grind, which means an even extraction and less bitterness.

Tip 3: Use filtered water

Coffee is 98% water. If your tap water tastes of chlorine, so will your coffee.

Tip 4: Mind the temperature

Aim for 92–96 °C. Boiling water scorches the grounds; let the kettle rest for 30 seconds.

Tip 5: Weigh your coffee

A ratio of 1:16 (one gram of coffee per sixteen grams of water) is a reliable starting point.

Tip 6: Pre-wet paper filters

Rinsing the filter removes papery flavours and warms up the brewer.

Tip 7: Clean your gear

Old coffee oils turn rancid. Wash your brewer and grinder hopper every week.
